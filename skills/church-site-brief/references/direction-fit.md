# Choosing directions

Recommend two or three directions, never one (the user should choose) and never all ten (that is not a recommendation). Score each direction: +1 for every strong signal the church matches, −2 for every poor-fit condition it matches. Take the top two or three. Break ties toward the safe fit (the one closest to what the church already is), and when sensible include one stretch that suits where the church wants to go (from the audience answer: young families, students, the neighbourhood). Say which is the safe fit and which is the stretch. Tradition names in the table (Lutheran, Episcopal) are signals, not rules: a small-town Lutheran church may still score highest on Small-Town Welcome.

The full direction files live in the church-site-design skill: `directions/NN-*.md`. Reference homepages: `examples/NN-*.html`.

| Direction | Strong signals | Poor fit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Cinematic Worship | Large or multisite; strong worship production; video footage; contemporary music | Small church; no video or worship photos; older congregation; liturgical worship |
| 02 Message-First Media | Teaching-centred; known preacher; podcast; large online audience; sermon series with artwork | Pastor-centred branding is unwelcome; no regular video; newcomers are the main audience |
| 03 Campaign Billboard | Event-rich calendar; anniversaries, revivals, festivals; historic Black church tradition; a designer or volunteer who makes graphics weekly | No one will update the campaign weekly; quiet or liturgical tradition |
| 04 Bright & Friendly Modern | Suburban; church plant; wants to feel approachable; newcomer-focused; coffee, groups, conversation | Strong liturgical identity; heritage building is the story |
| 05 Family-Forward Playful | Growing through young families; strong kids and student ministries; parents are the target | Older congregation; adults without kids are the main audience |
| 06 Editorial Quiet | Anglican, Reformed, Lutheran, Presbyterian; urban; thoughtful, word-centred; church year matters | Wants energy and excitement; mostly families with young kids |
| 07 Heritage Cathedral | Cathedral or historic downtown church; notable building, choir or organ; visitors and tourists | Modern building; no music program; small rural church |
| 08 Catholic Parish Hub | Catholic parish (also Orthodox, Episcopal or Lutheran with many weekly services); bilingual Masses; parish school | Non-sacramental tradition; one Sunday service |
| 09 Dark Moody Brand | Urban church plant; Gen Z and young adults; strong visual culture; night gatherings | Older congregation; families are the audience; accessibility concerns |
| 10 Small-Town Welcome | Small-town or rural; established congregation; older members; the building is a landmark; Wednesday suppers and choirs | Wants to look like a big-city church; urban church plant |

## Combinations that often fit

- Small-town church that wants more young families: 10 Small-Town Welcome (safe) and 05 Family-Forward Playful (stretch).
- Suburban nondenominational church: 04 Bright & Friendly Modern (safe), 01 Cinematic Worship if they have production, 05 if kids are the strength.
- Liturgical city parish: 06 Editorial Quiet (safe), 07 Heritage Cathedral if the building is historic.
- Catholic parish: 08 Catholic Parish Hub (safe), 07 Heritage Cathedral for a historic church, 06 Editorial Quiet for a small contemplative parish.
- Historic Black church: 03 Campaign Billboard (safe when someone updates it), 01 Cinematic Worship for large churches with video, 10 Small-Town Welcome for small rural congregations.
- Young-adult church plant: 09 Dark Moody Brand, 04 Bright & Friendly Modern.
- Teaching church with a podcast: 02 Message-First Media plus 04 or 01.

## Describing them to the user

Describe each in visitor terms, one line, with the reason it fits this church:

- "Small-Town Welcome: your building and Sunday times up front, a note from Pastor Daniel, large readable type. Fits a church where many members are older and the steeple is a landmark."
- "Family-Forward Playful: kids' groups and check-in front and centre, bright and friendly. Fits if you want to grow with young families."
