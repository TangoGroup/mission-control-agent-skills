# Church page catalog

What churches commonly need, why, and what each page holds. Use it to list what a church is missing. Suggest at most four additions at a time, in this order of value for most churches: Plan a Visit, Kids & Students, Watch, Next Steps, Prayer Request, Give, Events, Beliefs.

From the October 2026 study of 179 church sites: 87% put Give on the homepage, but only 47% offered a clear newcomer path (33% of small churches) and 46% a way to watch online (20% of small churches). The newcomer path and online worship are the most common gaps.

## Core (every church)

| Page | Why | Must hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | First impression and the weekly answer | Service times and address in the first screen; newcomer action; this week; watch; give; contact in footer |
| Plan a Visit / I'm New | The page that turns a visitor into a guest | Times, address, parking, door to use, what happens, how long, what to wear, kids on Sunday, accessibility, a "let us know you're coming" form, FAQ |
| About / Who We Are | Trust | Short story, beliefs summary or link, leadership, denomination or affiliation, history |
| Contact / Location | Getting there | Address, map link, phone, email, office hours |
| Give | Support | Online giving link (the church's provider), give by mail address, other ways (text, app, envelopes), year-end statements, campaigns |

## Common (most churches)

| Page | Why | Must hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kids & Students | Parents decide where the family goes | Age groups with times and places, check-in and safety, leaders, what kids do, how to sign up |
| Watch / Sermons | Online attendance, catching up, checking out the church | Livestream schedule and link, latest message, series archive, podcast links |
| Events / Calendar | Weekly rhythm and special days | Upcoming events with dates, times, places, sign-up; recurring weekly schedule |
| Next Steps | Moving from attender to member | Membership or newcomer class, baptism, groups, serving, giving |
| Groups / Classes | Belonging | Small groups, Sunday school classes, Bible studies, women's and men's ministries, how to join |
| Staff & Leadership | Faces and contacts | Photos, roles, short bios, how to reach each |
| Beliefs | Doctrinal clarity | Statement of faith in the church's own words (never written by you), denomination links |
| Prayer Request | Care | A short form (name optional), privacy choice, prayer meeting times |

## By tradition or size

| Page | When | Must hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mass Times / Worship Schedule | Catholic, Episcopal, Lutheran, Orthodox, cathedrals | Weekend and weekday services, Confession/Reconciliation, Adoration, holy days, language |
| Sacraments | Catholic, Anglican, Lutheran | Baptism, First Communion, Confirmation, Marriage, Anointing, Funerals: requirements and contacts |
| Bulletin | Liturgical churches, older congregations | This week's bulletin download and archive |
| Missions | Mission-giving churches | Partners, places, how to support or go |
| Music / Choir | Cathedrals, churches with music programs | Choirs, rehearsal times, concerts, organ |
| Visit & Tours | Historic buildings and cathedrals | Opening hours, tours, history, accessibility |
| Campuses / Locations | Multisite churches | Each campus with times, address, pastor |
| Español / languages | Bilingual congregations | Services in that language, key pages translated, a language toggle |
| School | Parish or church schools | Link to the school site, grades, enrollment |
| Rentals / Weddings | Churches renting space or hosting weddings | Spaces, policies, contact |
| Newsletter | Older or rural congregations | Latest newsletter and signup |

## Signals that a page is missing in substance

A page can exist and still fail: service times only in the footer; "Plan a Visit" that only repeats the address; a kids page that is only a logo; a sermon page that links to YouTube with no schedule; events from last year. Treat these as gaps.
