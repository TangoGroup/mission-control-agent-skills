A small parish keeping an old way of prayer.

We are an Anglican parish of about two hundred people near Laurelhurst. Every Sunday we read the Scriptures, pray the prayers of the Book of Common Prayer, and share bread and wine at one table. You are welcome to sit with us, listen, and take your time.

The order of worship

The shape of our service is very old and changes little from week to week. If you are new, you only need to follow the printed leaflet; the ushers will hand you one and point out where we are.

  1. The GatheringOpening hymn, the Collect for Purity, the Gloria
  2. The Word of GodReadings from the Old Testament, Psalm and Gospel; the sermon
  3. The Creed and the PrayersThe Nicene Creed, prayers for the world and the parish, confession
  4. The PeaceWe greet those around us; a handshake or a nod is fine
  5. The Holy CommunionAll baptized Christians may receive; others are welcome to come forward for a blessing
  6. The DismissalA closing hymn, then coffee in the hall

For visitors

If you have never been to a service like ours

You do not need to know when to stand or sit, and nobody will ask you to introduce yourself. Most people who come for the first time sit toward the back and watch. That is exactly right.

01Arriving

Come ten minutes early. There is street parking on Ankeny and 28th, and a bike rack by the red door. An usher will give you a leaflet with every word of the service printed in it.

02Children

Children are welcome in every service, wriggles and all. At 11:00 there is a children's chapel for ages 3 to 10, who rejoin their families at the Peace. The nursery is staffed from 8:45 to 12:30.

03Communion

All who are baptized may receive the bread and wine. If you would rather not, or are not baptized, cross your arms over your chest and the priest will say a short blessing.

04Afterward

Coffee is in the parish hall. If you would like to know more, Father Thomas keeps an hour every Thursday for anyone who wants to talk, about the faith or about nothing in particular.

— The Rev. Thomas Avery, Rector · Write to the rector

Formation & parish life

A parish is a school and a household. These are the places where people here learn, serve and get to know one another.

Catechism for adults

Sundays at 10:10 a.m., between the services. This term we are working through the Lord's Prayer line by line. For enquirers, the newly baptized, and anyone who has been coming for twenty years and never quite asked.

Notes from parish life

Children's chapel

Ages 3–10, Sundays at 11:00, with Godly Play stories.

Youth

Grades 6–12 meet Sunday evenings at the rectory, 5:30 to 7:30.

The choir

Twelve voices, rehearsing Thursdays at 7. No audition, just a willingness to read.

Supper groups

Six households, once a month, through Lent. Sign-up opens in January.

Ankeny Street pantry

Groceries for our neighbours every Saturday, 9 to 11. Volunteers always needed.

Altar guild

The quiet work of linens, candles and flowers. Tuesdays at 10.

Giving

The parish is kept by the people who pray here.

We have no endowment. Every candle, every bag of groceries at the pantry, and every hour of the rector's time is paid for by the gifts of this congregation. If you worship here, we ask you to give regularly and in proportion to what you have.
