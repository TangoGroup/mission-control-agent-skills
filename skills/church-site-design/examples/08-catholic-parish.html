Welcome · Bienvenidos

New to St. Anne? We are glad you found us.

Some of you were baptized at our font; others arrived last month from Laredo, Monterrey or Ohio. Our parish is made up of both, and we would not have it any other way. Come to any Mass, introduce yourself after, and let us know how we can help you settle in.

Fr. Miguel Ortega Pastor · Párroco
  1. 1

    Come to Mass

    Arrive ten minutes early; ushers at the main doors will help you find a seat. Parking is in the school lot on Calle Santa Ana.

  2. 2

    Register with the parish

    Registration lets us prepare your children for the sacraments, send your offering statements and visit you when you are sick.

  3. 3

    Find your place

    Sing in a choir, read at Mass, serve with St. Vincent de Paul or join a Bible study. There is room for everyone.

St. Anne Catholic School

Faith, learning and service, PK through 8th grade

Our parish school has taught the children of the West Side since 1924, in small classes with daily prayer, weekly school Mass and a bilingual program from Pre-K. Tuition assistance is available to every registered parish family.
