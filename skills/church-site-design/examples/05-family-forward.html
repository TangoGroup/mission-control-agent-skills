A Sunday with kids, step by step

Coming to church with little ones can feel like a lot. Here's the whole morning, so nobody has to guess (including the four-year-old).

  1. 1

    Park by the green doors

    Family parking with extra-wide spots for strollers and car seats sits right by the Creek Kids entrance.

    Aim for 8:45 or 10:15
  2. 2

    Check in at the kiosk

    A Creek Kids host helps you set up. Your child gets a name sticker, and you get a matching pickup tag.

    About 5 minutes the first time
  3. 3

    Drop off, go worship

    Walk your kids to their room and meet their leaders. If anyone needs you, we'll text your phone.

    Coffee's in the lobby
  4. 4

    Pick up and talk it over

    Show your tag at pickup. Kids bring home a Car Ride card with questions about the day's Bible story.

    Rooms close 15 min after service

Safe, checked and ready for your kids

You're trusting us with the most important people in your life. Here's how we take care of them.

  • Background checksEvery volunteer, renewed every two years, plus safety training.
  • Matching tagsKids only leave with the adult holding their pickup code.
  • Two adults, alwaysNo leader is ever alone with a child. Rooms have windows.
  • Allergy alertsPrinted right on the name tag. All snacks are peanut-free.
  • Secure hallwayOne staffed entrance to the kids wing during services.
  • Text, don't pageNeed you? Your phone buzzes. No numbers on the big screen.
