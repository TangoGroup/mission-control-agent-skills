Marcus & Jen Hale

Marcus and Jen started Northline in a borrowed brewery in 2014 with forty people and a folding table. Today Northline is a church of a few thousand across Denver and beyond, but they still teach the same way: open the Bible, tell the truth, make it livable on Monday.

They live in Park Hill with their three kids and a dog named Barnabas.

New here?

Ready to come in person?

Watched online for a while? Plenty of people find us through a sermon on their phone. When you're ready to visit, tell us you're coming and we'll meet you at the door.

Plan a Visit

  1. Park on Walnut, enter by the big garage doors

    Free parking in the lot on 31st St. Look for the blue Guest flags and a host with a lanyard.

  2. Grab coffee and check in the kids

    The café opens 30 minutes before each service. Northline Kids check-in is right off the lobby; we'll text you if we need you.

  3. About 70 minutes: songs, a message, no pressure

    Wear what you like. Sit where you like. Nobody will ask you to stand up or say anything.

  4. Meet us at Next Steps afterwards

    Say hi at the Welcome Bar, get a free copy of the series guide, and ask anything.
