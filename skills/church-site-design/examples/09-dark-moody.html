first time? here’s the whole thing.

  1. ParkStreet parking on Sunset, or the lot behind the building after 9:00.
  2. CoffeeFree, and actually good. Bar opens 30 minutes before each gathering.
  3. InsideAbout 75 minutes. A few songs, an honest talk, room to sit with it.
