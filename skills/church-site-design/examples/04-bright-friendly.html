I'm new here

Your first Sunday, start to finish

No guesswork. Here's exactly what happens from the parking lot to your seat, so you can spend your energy on the good stuff.

  1. 1

    Park in a Guest spot

    The yellow-signed spaces right by the main entrance are saved for first-timers. Our parking team will wave you in.

    Arrive around 8:45 or 10:30
  2. 2

    Say hi at the Welcome Desk

    Just inside the doors. We'll answer questions, give you a quick tour, and hand you a small thank-you gift.

    Takes two minutes
  3. 3

    Check in your kids

    A host walks you to Kids Check-In, prints matching name tags, and shows you their room. You keep the pickup tag.

    Pre-register to skip the line
  4. 4

    Grab a coffee, find a seat

    The coffee bar is free for guests, and the lattes are made by people who care a lot about lattes. Sit anywhere.

    Coffee bar from 8:30

Let us know you're coming

We'll save you a parking spot, have your kids' tags ready, and someone from our team will meet you at the door.

Good questions people ask

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see jeans, sweaters, the occasional Buckeyes jersey in November.

Is there anything for my kids?

Yes. Nursery (6 weeks to 2), Preschool, and Elementary rooms run at both services. Every volunteer is background-checked and trained.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. Giving is for people who call Common Ground home. Please consider yourself our guest.

Can I just text someone?

Absolutely. Text HELLO to (614) 555-0142 and a real person on our guest team will text you back.
