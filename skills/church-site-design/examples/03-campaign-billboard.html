First time?

Plan your visit

We're a traditional Black Baptist church with a big choir, warm ushers and a sermon you'll still be thinking about on Tuesday. Worship lasts about two hours, and nobody minds if you need to step out with a little one.

Let us know you're coming
1125 New Hope BlvdMemphis, TN 38106 · Free parking in the east lot and across Hope Ave
Get directions
  1. 1

    Park & enter

    Parking attendants in orange vests will wave you in. Use the main doors under the bell tower.

  2. 2

    Meet an usher

    Our ushers will seat you and hand you a program. Visitor cards are in the pew rack.

  3. 3

    Worship with us

    Praise and worship, the Mass Choir, prayer, the Word, and an invitation to discipleship.

  4. 4

    Stay for fellowship

    Visit the Welcome Table in the vestibule after service. New Members Class meets the first Sunday at 9 a.m.
