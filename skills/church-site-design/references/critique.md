# Looking at a church site

Judge only the screenshots, as a visitor would see them. Look at the homepage at desktop and phone first, then every other page at phone width (most church visitors arrive on phones, often from a Facebook link on Sunday morning).

## Five questions, in order

1. **Can a newcomer answer "When, where, and what do I do with my kids?" from the first phone screen?** If not, nothing else matters yet.
2. **Does it look like this church and no other?** Its building, its people, its name, its words. Swap in the church down the road: if nothing changes but the name, it is a template.
3. **Is it the best version of its direction?** Compare with the direction file's blueprint and the reference homepage. Is the hero as described, are the signature components present, are the pitfalls avoided?
4. **Can an older member read it?** Body size, contrast, tap targets, no light-grey text, no text over busy photos.
5. **Is it finished?** No placeholder, no stretched or blurry photos, no empty sections, no stale events, every link goes somewhere.

## Score (0–10 each)

| Criterion | 10 looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Clarity | Times, place, newcomer action and kids answer are instantly findable on phone and desktop |
| Belonging | It could only be this church: own photos or well-chosen stock, own words, own details |
| Direction | Unmistakably its direction, executed with care; signature components present |
| Craft | Type scale, spacing, alignment, image crops and colour all deliberate; consistent across pages |
| Access | Readable at arm's length on a phone; keyboard and screen-reader friendly |

Caps: Clarity under 6 caps the total at 6. Any placeholder or broken image caps Craft at 4. Text over a busy photo without a scrim caps Access at 5.

Write the scores and the three biggest problems, fix those, re-check, look again. Three rounds at most.

## Common failures

- The hero is beautiful and the times are three screens down.
- A row of six identical icon cards for ministries.
- Carousel heroes: half the visitors never see slide two; use one hero.
- Staff photos of different sizes and crops in one grid.
- Stock photos that contradict the church: a cathedral interior for a Baptist church, saints' statues behind a nondenominational welcome, a big-city skyline for a rural town.
- Footer too small to read, though it is where members look for the phone number.
