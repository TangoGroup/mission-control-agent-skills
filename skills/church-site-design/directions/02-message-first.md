# 02 Message-First Media

The latest sermon is the front door. The homepage reads like a media company: a big video player, series artwork, episode lists, a podcast, a devotional email, the lead pastor's books. Newcomer information is present but second.

## Choose it when

Teaching-centred church; a recognised preacher; a podcast or large online audience; sermon series with artwork; much of the congregation watches online.

## Avoid it when

The church does not want pastor-centred branding; sermons are not recorded regularly; newcomers and families are the main audience (pair with 04 instead).

## Studied examples

Churchome (churchome.org), Saddleback (saddleback.com), Harvest (harvest.org), World Changers (worldchangers.org), Fellowship Church (fellowshipchurch.com), Fresh Life (freshlife.church), Calvary Chapel (calvarycch.org), North Point (northpoint.org).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Live bar** on top: "Next livestream Sunday 9:30 a.m. MT · starts in 1d 4h" with a pulsing live dot only while live.
2. **Header**: wordmark; Watch, Podcast, Series, Devotional, I'm New, Events; Plan a Visit and Give.
3. **Hero**: "Watch the latest message" headline; a large 16:9 video card (thumbnail, play button, title, speaker, scripture, date, length) beside the current series panel (CSS-designed series art, episode list with the current one marked "Now playing"); actions: Watch full service, Listen on the podcast, Sermon notes.
4. **Times band**: Sundays, location, kids and students, first time.
5. **Recent messages** row and **one-minute shorts** row (vertical clip cards), like a video platform.
6. **Podcast**: show art, latest episodes, platform links (Apple, Spotify, YouTube).
7. **Series archive**: grid of series covers designed in CSS (type-led art, each with its own palette).
8. **Devotional signup**: email form for the daily devotional.
9. **The pastors**: portrait or studio photo, short bio, book or resources.
10. **Stream schedule**, then a modest **newcomer path**, ministries, events, giving, footer.

## Type

A confident editorial sans for interface and headlines, with a serif for series titles and accents.
- Schibsted Grotesk + Instrument Serif italics (reference build).
- Manrope + Fraunces; Hanken Grotesk + Newsreader.

## Colour

Clean ink and off-white; colour comes from series artwork, each series its own palette; one interface accent; a live red used only for the live dot.
- Reference: ink #111214, paper #f4f2ed, cobalt #2f4fe0, night navy #0f1b3d; series art sage #2f3b2d, sand #e9dfc9, amber #e3a33b; live #e0412f.

## Photos

Preaching on stage (several angles), the podcast set, the pastors in a studio portrait, congregation listening. Never caption a stranger as the lead pastor; credit stock speakers as guest or teaching pastors, or use the church's real photos.

## Motion

Hover on video cards (thumbnail scale 1.03, play button grows), the live dot pulse, horizontal scroll rows with snap. Little else.

## Signature components

Video player card, episode list, shorts row, podcast platform row, series cover grid, devotional signup, stream schedule.

## Pitfalls

Newcomers cannot find service times (keep them in a band right under the hero); the church starts to look like one person's brand; embedding YouTube players (link out with styled thumbnails instead); stale "latest" message.
