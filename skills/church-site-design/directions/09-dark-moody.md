# 09 Dark Moody Brand

A lifestyle brand that happens to be a church. Near-black pages, black-and-white portraits, few words, lots of space, fine grain, and a fashion-editorial confidence. Aimed squarely at Gen Z and young adults in cities.

## Choose it when

An urban church plant or young-adult church; a strong visual culture (photographers, designers in the congregation); night gatherings; merch, podcast, worship nights.

## Avoid it when

An older congregation; families are the audience; accessibility is a priority you cannot meet in dark mode (keep contrast high if you choose it anyway).

## Studied examples

VOUS Church (vouschurch.com), Zoe Church (zoechurch.org), CCV (ccv.church), Churchome (churchome.org), Legacy Hills (ChurchCo Waves theme), Union Chapel (Nucleus), Bethel (Webflow template), Haven (Framer template).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Header**: small wordmark (lowercase with a mark); Sundays, Honest (the series), Circles (groups), Nights, Shop; Give; New Here (outlined).
2. **Hero**: very dark ground, a black-and-white portrait offset to one side, a short line in a large serif, lowercase with an italic turn ("come as / *you are.*"), and one quiet line: "Sundays 10:00 · 12:30 · 7:00 pm · The Echo Room, 2100 Sunset Blvd".
3. **A statement** in large serif, three lines at most ("For the doubters, the devoted and the barely holding on.").
4. **Sundays**: big serif times with a first-time list (what to expect in five lines) and a "let us know you're coming" form.
5. **The series**: full-width photograph with the series name and an episode list.
6. **Podcast** row.
7. **People**: staggered black-and-white portraits, first names and a word each (only real people or clearly stock).
8. **Nights**: worship night with a short calendar.
9. **Shop**: three merch tiles (drawn in SVG if no product photos).
10. **Giving** (flip to a light section for contrast), **footer**.

## Type

A refined display serif or a condensed sans in caps, paired with a sharp grotesk for small wide-tracked labels and text.
- Instrument Serif (lowercase, italics) + Hanken Grotesk (reference build).
- Six Caps or Bebas Neue + Archivo; Fraunces (soft) + Inter Tight.

## Colour

Black, bone, one restrained accent; one light section for rhythm.
- Reference: black #0b0b0a, bone #ece7de, steel blue #93a8bd.
- Alternatives: warm amber #c8924a on black; deep oxblood on black. Avoid the acid-green or vermilion-on-black cliché.

## Photos

Black-and-white or heavily graded: portraits, crowds at worship nights, the city at night. Crop carefully (shoulders and up for portraits). Fine animated grain over the page is optional and must stay subtle.

## Motion

Slow, sparse: grain, fades, a hover that brightens a portrait. No bounce.

## Signature components

Lowercase serif hero, one-line Sundays, staggered portraits, series panel, podcast row, nights calendar, merch tiles.

## Pitfalls

Low contrast and tiny type (keep body 17px+ in bone on black, labels 13px+); mystery navigation (keep plain labels); too few words for a newcomer to know when to come; older visitors leaving. Always keep times and address in the first screen.
