# 06 Editorial Quiet

A well-made book. Serif headlines, paper-toned pages, generous space and a few still photographs set like plates. The words carry the page: who we are, how we worship, what season of the church year it is, what was preached. Calm, intelligent, intimate.

## Choose it when

Anglican, Episcopal, Lutheran, Presbyterian, Reformed or other word-and-sacrament congregations; urban or university-town parishes; the church year and the order of worship matter; the church wants to feel thoughtful rather than loud.

## Avoid it when

The church wants energy and excitement; mostly young families; newcomers unfamiliar with church language (explain terms, or choose 04).

## Studied examples

Grace Community Church (gracechurch.org), Fresh Life (freshlife.church, newspaper layout), Redeemer Presbyterian (redeemer.com), City Church San Francisco (citychurchsf.org), Downtown Presbyterian Nashville (downtownpres.org), Emmanuel Anglican NYC (emmanuelanglicannyc.com), Squarespace church templates (Gamma, Alpha).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Header**: small wordmark with a quiet mark (cross, monogram); Worship, Sermons, Formation, Calendar; "Visiting? Start here"; Give.
2. **Church year line** under the header: "The Season after Pentecost · This Sunday, October 18: the Twenty-first Sunday after Pentecost and the Feast of St. Luke · Advent begins November 29".
3. **Hero**: a large serif statement in a narrow measure ("A small parish keeping an old way of prayer."), a paragraph in the church's own voice, two text links (Planning a first visit, This Sunday's order of service); one photograph set like a plate with a small caption ("Plate I · The nave, a Tuesday afternoon").
4. **When we gather**: services set like a table of contents (day, service, short description in italics, time right-aligned); address and directions.
5. **This Sunday**: the order of worship (a real ordered list, Roman numerals if the tradition uses them), the readings, the preacher and sermon title.
6. **Church year strip**: seasons as a quiet colour band (green for Ordinary Time, violet for Advent).
7. **Visiting**: a short essay from the rector or pastor (only the church's words, or a draft marked for approval).
8. **Sermons** as a clean list (date, title, text, preacher), linked to audio.
9. **Formation**: catechism or adult class, a reading list.
10. **Parish life** notes, **calendar** (All Saints', Lessons and Carols), **giving**, newsletter signup, **footer**.

## Type

A refined text serif for display (light weights, large), a quiet sans or the same serif for interface and times.
- Newsreader (light) + Hanken Grotesk for labels and times (reference build).
- EB Garamond + Inter Tight small sizes; Cormorant Garamond + Source Sans 3; Libre Caslon + Work Sans.

## Colour

Paper, ink, one muted accent. Liturgical colours appear only where the season is named.
- Reference: paper #f2efe8, second paper #e7e3da, ink #1f1d1a / #4a463f, deep green #2e4a3b; Advent violet and muted gold only in the church year strip.
- Alternatives: white + ink + oxblood #6b2230; stone #eeece6 + slate blue #34495e. Never terracotta on cream (the most common AI default).

## Photos

Still, natural light: bread and cup, an open book, the nave empty, stained glass, hands. Few, large, captioned. Black-and-white works.

## Motion

Almost none: a soft fade on the hero. Stillness is the point.

## Signature components

Church year line and strip, services as a table of contents, order of worship, sermon list, reading list, plate captions.

## Pitfalls

Cold or unfinished for newcomers (keep a clear "Visiting? Start here"); tiny light-grey text; long unbroken paragraphs; church jargon unexplained; looking like a generic AI editorial template (avoid hairline-ruled broadsheet columns everywhere; one or two tables only).
