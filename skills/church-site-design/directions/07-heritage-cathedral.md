# 07 Heritage Cathedral

The building and its music are the story. Architecture photography (nave, vaults, stained glass), stately titling type and deep jewel tones, with worship, choral music, visiting and history given equal weight for worshippers and for visitors. Dignified, civic, timeless.

## Choose it when

A cathedral, a historic downtown church, or a congregation with a notable building, choir or organ; tourists and concertgoers visit; a capital or restoration campaign.

## Avoid it when

A modern building; no music programme; a small rural church (use 10); the church wants to feel young and informal.

## Studied examples

St. Patrick's Cathedral (saintpatrickscathedral.org), St. Thomas Fifth Avenue (saintthomaschurch.org), St. Bart's (stbarts.org), Washington National Cathedral (cathedral.org), St. John the Divine (stjohndivine.org), Grace Cathedral (gracecathedral.org), Fifth Avenue Presbyterian (fapc.org), St Paul's London (stpauls.co.uk).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Top bar**: "Open today 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. · Choral Evensong this Sunday at 4:00 p.m."; Watch live; Contact.
2. **Header**: crest and name in titling caps with "Pittsburgh · Founded 1872"; Worship, Music, Calendar, History; Plan your visit (outline), Give.
3. **Hero**: full-width interior photograph with slow zoom, a small italic line ("Episcopal · In the heart of Pittsburgh since 1872"), a titling headline ("A House of Prayer for All People"), one sentence, Plan your visit and Times of worship.
4. **Today at the cathedral** band (navy): doors, Sunday Eucharists, Evensong, Saturday tour.
5. **The daily round of prayer**: a schedule (daily and Sunday) beside a red card with this Sunday's Evensong music (responses, canticles, anthem, voluntary).
6. **Plan your visit**: two columns, for worshippers and for visitors (tours, accessibility, what to see), with a drawn map.
7. **Music**: choir and organ over a light-filled photograph; recital series.
8. **Concerts and seasonal calendar** with the major service featured (Lessons and Carols).
9. **Newcomer path and ministries**.
10. **History timeline** beside a vault photograph.
11. **Watch and listen** (modest), **campaign** with progress only from real figures, **giving**, newsletter, **footer**.

## Type

Titling serif caps for headings with a readable book serif for text; a touch of blackletter is allowed for a single wordmark in Gothic Revival settings.
- Cinzel + EB Garamond (reference build).
- Cormorant SC + Cormorant Garamond; Marcellus + Crimson Pro.

## Colour

Deep jewel tones with metal and stone: one dark field (navy or deep red), gold for rules and accents, parchment and stone grounds for reading sections.
- Reference: navy #0c1427 / #13203d, deep red #7a1c2a, gold #c9a54f / #e3cb8c, parchment #f3ecdc, stone #e7dcc4.
- Alternatives: oxblood + gold + limestone; forest green + brass + parchment.

## Photos

The church's own architecture photos above all: nave, vaulting, windows, choir stalls, organ, the exterior at dusk. Check stock stained glass for text or sponsor names in the glass and crop them out.

## Motion

Slow zoom on the hero (20s), restrained entrances. Nothing playful.

## Signature components

Today at the cathedral, daily schedule, music list for the next choral service, visitor and worshipper paths, history timeline, campaign.

## Pitfalls

A museum site where newcomers cannot tell when worship happens; titling caps at small sizes (unreadable); too many dark sections (keep parchment reading areas); low-resolution interior photos stretched full width.
