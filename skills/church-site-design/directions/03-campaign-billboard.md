# 03 Campaign Billboard

The hero is a designed poster for what is happening now: an anniversary homecoming, a revival, a festival, a series. Bold display type, saturated colour, cut-out photos, dates and a seal. Below, an announcement ticker, a worship schedule band and a calendar-led homepage that changes with the church year. Very common on historic Black church sites and in multisite seasonal campaigns.

## Choose it when

Event-rich calendar; anniversaries, revivals, conferences, festivals; historic Black church tradition; someone at the church will update the campaign every few weeks.

## Avoid it when

No one will update it (a stale poster is worse than none); a quiet or liturgical tradition; the church wants a calm, timeless site.

## Studied examples

Life.Church (life.church), New Birth (newbirth.org), Greater Allen AME (allencathedral.org), Alfred Street Baptist (alfredstreet.org), Trinity UCC Chicago (trinitychicago.org), Ebenezer Baptist (ebenezeratl.org), Crossroads (crossroads.net), Bridgeway Church (Church Plant Media).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Top strip**: Watch Live times; phone; I'm New; Give.
2. **Header**: seal or wordmark with the full church name; Homecoming (the campaign), Our Pastor, Ministries, Events, I'm New; Plan Your Visit, Watch Live, Give.
3. **Hero poster** (built in HTML/CSS, not an image): church name and years in a small line; a giant number or word in the display face (e.g. "125"); the campaign title stacked in condensed caps; a serif italic subtitle; a dark date bar ("Nov 13–15, 2026 · Memphis, Tennessee"); two actions (See the weekend, RSVP); photos in shaped frames (arch, circle) with an offset colour shape behind; a rotating seal ("125 · Est. 1901"); three date blocks for each day's event; a patterned stripe (kente-inspired or seasonal).
4. **Announcements ticker** in the accent colour (pauses on hover, wraps under reduced motion).
5. **Worship Schedule band**: Sunday School, Morning Worship, Bible Study as big times.
6. **What's happening**: campaign tiles of different sizes (food drive with a goal, Watch Night, theme of the year).
7. **Watch Live** with countdown; **Pastor's welcome** (pastor and first lady, portrait, a welcome the church provides).
8. **Ministries** as colour-field cards (Mass Choir, Men of Valor, Women of Virtue, Youth, Nursing Home Ministry, Scholarship Fund).
9. **New here** steps, **events** with date blocks, **giving** (campaign meter only with real figures), **footer**.

## Type

A striking display face with a clean text face.
- DM Serif Display + Archivo (variable width for condensed caps) (reference build).
- Abril Fatface or Alfa Slab One + Work Sans; Bebas Neue + Source Sans 3.

## Colour

Rich and saturated, but a controlled set of four or five: one dominant field colour, one metal (gold), one deep counter colour, ivory, ink.
- Reference: crimson #9b1a30 (dark #6c0f21, #4a0916), gold #e9b44c / #f6d58a, deep purple #2b1342, ivory #fbf4e6, ink #1c0d15, stripe green #1d6a44.
- Alternatives: royal purple + gold + white; emerald + gold + black; a seasonal palette per campaign.

## Photos

Choirs in robes, worship leaders singing, raised hands, joyful congregation, the pastor and first lady (ask for real portraits; stock cannot stand in for a named pastor), the building for anniversaries.

## Motion

The ticker; a slowly rotating seal; entrances on campaign tiles. Countdown to the event or the live service.

## Signature components

Poster hero, ticker, worship schedule band, campaign tiles, date blocks, pastor and first lady welcome, ministry colour fields.

## Pitfalls

A wall of flyers (one campaign leads, others are tiles); flyers saved as images with text baked in (unreadable, inaccessible: build them in HTML); the campaign going stale; clashing colours from different flyers.
