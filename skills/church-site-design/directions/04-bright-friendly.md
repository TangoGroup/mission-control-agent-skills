# 04 Bright & Friendly Modern

Light, open and conversational. Candid photos of people in rounded frames, white ground with a few clear brand colours in blocks, a friendly sans, and a step-by-step newcomer path that answers a nervous visitor's questions before they ask. Grown-up and warm, not corporate.

## Choose it when

Suburban church or church plant; wants to feel approachable; newcomer-focused; coffee, groups, tables and conversation are the culture.

## Avoid it when

The liturgy or the building is the story (06, 07, 08); kids are the main focus (05).

## Studied examples

Menlo Church (menlo.church), Willow Creek (willowcreek.org), Christ Fellowship (gochristfellowship.com), Eagle Brook (eaglebrookchurch.com), The Village Church (thevillagechurch.net), Twin Lakes Church (tlc.org), Highrock (highrock.org), Creekside (Ministry Designs).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Header** (white, sticky): wordmark; Sundays, Find your place, Groups, Messages, Events; Give (outline pill), I'm new here (filled pill).
2. **Hero**: a short, warm headline ("Pull up a chair. We saved you one."), a chat-style welcome from the lead pastor (two or three message bubbles), actions (Plan your first Sunday, Watch this week's message); a rounded candid photo (coffee, conversation) with a floating times card ("This Sunday, Oct 18 · 9:00 & 10:45 a.m. · address").
3. **Three facts strip**: how long, kids are covered, where to park.
4. **Your first Sunday, start to finish**: four numbered steps (parking, welcome desk, kids check-in, coffee and a seat), each with a practical detail; then a plan-a-visit form beside a questions accordion.
5. **Find your place**: two big photo cards (Groups, Tables and meals) plus smaller cards (Kids, Students, Young Adults, Serve).
6. **Groups finder**: filter chips by night and type, working in script, with real group cards.
7. **Latest message** as a modest card.
8. **Events** (Serve Day, potluck, newcomer lunch, Christmas Eve times).
9. **Giving**, then a dark **footer** with a drawn map.

## Type

A friendly geometric or humanist sans with personality, one family.
- Plus Jakarta Sans 400–800, display at 800 with tight tracking (reference build).
- Outfit; Manrope; Rethink Sans; DM Sans + DM Serif Display for one accent.

## Colour

White ground, ink text, one deep brand colour for buttons and dark sections, one warm accent, light tints for blocks.
- Reference: white, ink #16211f, deep teal #0e5650, marigold #f4b740; tints mint #d6ede6, sky #dce9f7, light yellow #fff1cf, mist #f3f5f2.
- Alternatives: forest green + coral; navy + sunflower; the church's brand mapped onto these roles. Avoid purple-to-blue gradients.

## Photos

Candid conversation over coffee, tables of people eating together, small groups in living rooms, volunteers greeting at the door. Rounded corners (20–28px), one radius site-wide.

## Motion

Gentle entrances; chat bubbles appear in sequence once; hover lift on cards (small); chips animate selection.

## Signature components

Chat-style welcome, floating times card, first-Sunday steps, plan-a-visit form + FAQ, Find your place cards, groups finder.

## Pitfalls

Drifting into a generic SaaS landing page; every section a row of identical cards (vary sizes, use one list, one photo split); stock that looks like an office; vague copy ("community", "journey") instead of specifics (where to park, how long).
