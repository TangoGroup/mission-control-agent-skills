# 01 Cinematic Worship

The site sells the feeling of being in the room on Sunday. A full-screen worship photo or video under a dark overlay, a huge white headline, two buttons (Plan a Visit, Watch Live), then big photo-led sections alternating dark and light. Megachurch production value: countdowns, campus finders, series branding.

Most common pattern among large churches in the study: 80% of megachurch homepages open on full-bleed media, 68% use video.

## Choose it when

Large or multisite church; strong worship production; real worship photos or video; contemporary music; a sermon series with its own title.

## Avoid it when

Small church without production photos (stock concert crowds will look fake); liturgical worship; an older congregation; slow rural connections (heavy video).

## Studied examples

Gateway Church (gatewaypeople.com), Mosaic (mosaic.org), Lakewood (lakewoodchurch.com), NewSpring (newspring.cc), Bellevue Baptist (bellevue.org), Austin Stone (austinstone.org), Planetshakers (planetshakers.com), Nucleus customer sites (Village Church, Union Chapel).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Transparent header over the hero**: wordmark left; Campuses, Next Steps, Ministries, Watch, Events; Give (outline) and Plan a Visit (filled accent).
2. **Hero, 100svh**: worship photo or muted looping video, slow ken-burns, a dark scrim (bottom-weighted), a small series label, the series or welcome headline set huge (often all caps, condensed, 1–2 words per line), one sentence, Plan a Visit + Watch Live. Under it on the hero: "Next live service" with a countdown (days, hours, minutes) and a reminder link.
3. **Times band** (dark): Saturday and Sunday times, online, campus count.
4. **Latest message** (dark): large video thumbnail with play button, series art, episode title, speaker, scripture.
5. **Find a campus** (light): campus cards or a campus list beside a drawn map; each with times and address.
6. **Next Steps** (light): Starting Point class, Groups, Baptism, Dream Team (serve) as big photo tiles of uneven sizes.
7. **Ministries**: Kids, Students, Young Adults, Groups with photos.
8. **Events carousel** (horizontal scroll with snap, or a 3-up grid on desktop).
9. **App band**: phone mockup drawn in CSS, store links.
10. **Giving** band, **footer** (dark).

## Type

Heavy or condensed grotesk for display, all caps, tight tracking; the same family or a plain grotesk for text.
- Archivo (compressed width 72%, weight 900) for both (reference build).
- Anton or Big Shoulders Display + Barlow.
- Oswald 700 + Inter Tight is common in the wild; prefer one of the above.

## Colour

Near-black ground for hero and alternating sections, white text, one bright accent on buttons and live dots only; light sections in off-white.
- Reference: #08090b, #ffffff, #f4f4f2, accent sun yellow #ffd23f (black text on it).
- Alternatives: electric blue #2f6bff; warm coral #ff6a4d; the church's brand colour if it is bright.

## Photos

Wide worship crowds with raised hands and stage light; the band; baptisms; people laughing in the lobby. The church's own production photos are far better than stock: ask for them. Video: a 10–20s muted loop, under 6 MB, with a poster image.

## Motion

Slow ken-burns on the hero (12–20s), a light sweep, staggered entrances, a ticking countdown. Pause video when off screen; nothing autoplays with sound.

## Signature components

Countdown to next live service; Find a Campus; series branding; Next Steps tiles; app download band.

## Pitfalls

Generic stock-concert hero that could be any church; text over busy video without a scrim; heavy pages on phones (compress, lazy-load); hiding service times below the fold; carousels in the hero.
