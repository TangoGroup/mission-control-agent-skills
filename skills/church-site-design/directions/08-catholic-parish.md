# 08 Catholic Parish Hub

Mass times are the hero. An organised, information-rich parish home: a Mass and Confession times panel beside a sanctuary photograph, sacraments navigation, the bulletin, parish news and calendar, faith formation, the school, often in two languages. Calm and orderly, never a portal of boxes.

## Choose it when

A Catholic parish; also Orthodox, Episcopal or Lutheran parishes with many weekly services; bilingual Masses; a parish school; many ministries and sacramental programmes.

## Avoid it when

A non-sacramental tradition with one Sunday service (use 04 or 10).

## Studied examples

St. Matthew Catholic, Topeka (saintmatthews.org), Catholic Shrine of the Immaculate Conception, Atlanta (catholicshrineatlanta.org), St. Dominic, Denver (stdominicdenver.org), St. Mary Parish, Appleton (stmaryparish.org, Nucleus), St. Monica, Santa Monica (stmonica.net), eCatholic templates (Baritus, Calvary), Diocesan templates (Alphonsus).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Utility bar**: liturgical season dot and name ("Ordinary Time · 29th Week"), this week's bulletin, school, register, contact, EN | ES toggle.
2. **Header**: parish crest (shield with a patron saint's symbol) and name; Mass Times, Sacraments, Faith Formation, School, News & Bulletin; New Parishioners (outline), Give.
3. **Hero**: parish name in a classic serif, a bilingual subtitle ("Parroquia Santa Ana · Founded 1921"), a one-line identity in italics, two or three sentences of welcome, address with directions, Register as a parishioner and Watch Mass online; beside or over the sanctuary photo, the **Mass Times panel**: Next Mass line computed live; weekend Masses with language tags; weekday Masses; Confession; Adoration; this week's holy day; link to the full schedule.
4. **Sacraments strip**: Baptism, Reconciliation, First Communion, Confirmation, Marriage, Holy Orders, Anointing of the Sick, each a simple line icon and a link.
5. **Welcome from the pastor** (text the parish provides) with three steps (come to Mass, register, find your place) and a **new parishioner registration** form.
6. **Bulletin and news**: download this week's bulletin, news items, events list (feasts, festivals, penance services).
7. **Adoration and the latest homily**.
8. **Faith formation and ministries** (OCIA, religious education, youth, St. Vincent de Paul, choirs).
9. **Parish school** band with enrollment.
10. **Giving** (online, envelopes, a campaign with real figures), **footer** with full schedule and office hours.

## Type

A classic serif for titles with a highly legible sans for schedules.
- Cormorant Garamond + Source Sans 3 (reference build).
- Crimson Pro + Public Sans; Spectral + Figtree.

## Colour

Marian navy and white with gold, the liturgical season as a small accent (green in Ordinary Time, violet in Advent and Lent, white and gold at Christmas and Easter).
- Reference: navy #17305a / #0f2242, gold #c19a48 (dark text gold #7a5b18), blue-grey #e9eff8, warm white #fbfaf6, season green #2e6a3e.
- Alternatives: deep red + gold + cream for older parishes; white + Marian blue + silver for modern buildings.

## Photos

Mass with the congregation, the priest at the altar (elevation, chalice), candles, the sanctuary, parish festivals, the school. Never photos of a real named priest captioned as the parish priest unless they are.

## Motion

Minimal. The Next Mass line updates; the language toggle switches smoothly.

## Signature components

Mass Times panel with Next Mass, sacraments strip, bulletin download, registration form, EN | ES toggle (swaps labels and `html lang`, remembered per visitor), liturgical season indicator.

## Pitfalls

A cluttered grid of boxes and sliders (most real parish sites); schedules unreadable on phones (put the panel right after the title on mobile, large times, language tags); out-of-date bulletins and holy days; the toggle translating only the menu.
