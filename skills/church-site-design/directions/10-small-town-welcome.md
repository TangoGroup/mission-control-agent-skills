# 10 Small-Town Welcome

The building is the hero and the welcome is personal. The church exterior, "Welcome to First Baptist", service times in plain view, a note from the pastor, Wednesday supper, the prayer list, the founding year, done with large readable type for a congregation that skews older. Warm, plain-spoken and trustworthy, and the best version of a site most small churches have only seen done badly.

From the study: small-church sites were the weakest on newcomer paths (33%) and online worship (20%), and most were dated templates with clip art, script fonts and walls of text. This direction keeps their warmth and fixes the rest.

## Choose it when

A small-town or rural church; an established congregation with older members; the building is a landmark; Wednesday suppers, choirs, VBS and potlucks are the life of the church.

## Avoid it when

The church wants to look like a big-city or megachurch site; an urban church plant.

## Studied examples

First Baptist Oxford PA (fbcoxford.org), First Baptist Woodstock AL (fbcwoodstock.org), Brenham First UMC (fumcbrenham.org), Decorah Lutheran (decorahlutheran.org), Harpeth Hills Church of Christ (harpethhills.org), Mulvane Church of Christ (mulvanechurch.com), Faith Bible Spearfish (fbcspearfish.org), Mount Olive Lutheran (mtolivelutheran.org).

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Top**: name and "of Pine Ridge, Missouri · Since 1887" with a small steeple mark; phone and office hours; a working "Larger text" button; Give.
2. **Solid coloured nav bar** (brick red): up to seven page links, for example About Us, Ministries, Calendar, Sermons, Prayer Requests, Contact (the wordmark already goes home; Give sits in the top row); Plan Your Visit button.
3. **Hero**: "Welcome to First Baptist Pine Ridge" in a sturdy serif, a warm paragraph in the church's voice ("We're the white church on the hill at Main and Walnut..."), a Sunday times box (Sunday School 9:30, Morning Worship 10:45) with the address and directions; actions (Plan your first Sunday, All service times); the church exterior photo with a founding-year badge ("1887 · 139 years") and a caption.
4. **Service times band** (forest green): Sunday School, Morning Worship, Wednesday supper and prayer meeting, youth; "Can't be here? Watch on Facebook" with a button.
5. **A word from our pastor**: photo (the steeple or the pastor), a signed note (the church's words or an approved draft).
6. **Your first Sunday**: questions and answers (parking, which door, accessibility, what to wear, kids, how long).
7. **Ministries**, led by Wednesday Night Supper with the potluck photo and this week's menu; Sunday School classes; choir; women's circle; youth; VBS.
8. **Calendar** (Harvest Festival, Thanksgiving dinner, Christmas cantata, Christmas Eve) and a weekly schedule; a mission offering with a goal only from real figures.
9. **Latest sermon** card (modest), **prayer request** form and the prayer chain phone line.
10. **Our history** timeline from the founding year.
11. **Giving** (online, the plate on Sunday, by mail with the address), **map and office hours**, a large legible **footer**.

## Type

A sturdy friendly serif or slab for headings and a very legible sans at 19px for text.
- Bitter + Nunito Sans at about 19px (reference build). Atkinson Hyperlegible was tried and rejected: its slashed zeros make times look technical.
- Merriweather + Source Sans 3; Zilla Slab + Public Sans; Libre Baskerville + Lato.

## Colour

Warm traditional colours: one strong bar colour, one deep band colour, navy text, warm white.
- Reference: brick red #8e2b22 (nav bar, date blocks), forest green #2c4a37 (service band, footer), navy #1d2a3b (text), warm white #fbfaf7, stone #f1eee8, pale brick #f6e9e5 (prayer requests).
- Alternatives: navy bar + cream + gold; forest green + brick + cream; the church's existing colours, cleaned up.

## Photos

When the only good building photo is small (common: 450–960px), keep the split hero and show the photo in a framed panel at or below its real width rather than full-bleed; the check fails photos stretched past 1.3×. The building in its best season (autumn, snow, spring), the steeple, the sanctuary full on a Sunday, Wednesday supper, choir, VBS kids, the pastor. The church's own photos are almost always available here: use them, even if small (show them smaller rather than blurry). Avoid stock that contradicts the tradition (no saints for a Baptist church, no city skylines).

## Motion

Almost none. Gentle reveals at most. Respect reduced motion; older visitors find movement distracting.

## Signature components

Larger-text button, founding-year badge, service-times band, pastor's note, first-Sunday Q&A, Wednesday supper menu, prayer request with prayer chain phone, history timeline, give by mail.

## Pitfalls

Dated looks (clip art, script fonts, centred walls of text, colourful circle buttons, sliders); small text; burying the phone number; stale events; using big-church language ("campuses", "experiences") that does not fit.
