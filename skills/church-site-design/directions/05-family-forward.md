# 05 Family-Forward Playful

Kids and parents come first. Bright near-primary colours on white, playful shapes (arches, circles, waves), rounded display type, and a homepage built around kids' age groups, safe check-in and family events. Written for the parent deciding where to bring their children, joyful without being childish.

## Choose it when

The church is growing through young families; kids and student ministries are a strength; parents are the people the site must win.

## Avoid it when

The congregation is mostly older; adults without children are the main audience; a quiet or liturgical tradition (pair 05's kids page with another direction's homepage instead).

## Studied examples

Church of the Highlands (churchofthehighlands.com), Life.Church (life.church), Woodland Hills Church (whchurch.org), St Aldates Oxford (staldates.org.uk), Church of the Nativity (churchnativity.com), Tithe.ly Jubilee theme.

## Homepage blueprint

1. **Announcement bar** for the next family event.
2. **Header**: wordmark; Creek Kids (the kids ministry name), Students, Parents, Events, Watch; Give, Plan your visit.
3. **Hero**: a parent-facing headline ("Sundays your kids will ask for."), one sentence, actions (Pre-register your kids, What Sunday looks like), small chips with times and address; a family photo in an arch frame with a sun circle, a squiggle and a sticker ("Nursery to 12th grade").
4. **Times band** in the sunshine colour: services, kids' check-in times, address.
5. **A Sunday with kids, step by step**: four steps along a dotted path (park by the green doors, check in at the kiosk, drop off and go to worship, pick up and talk it over).
6. **Kids ministry**: three age cards (Nursery 0–2, Preschool 3–5, Elementary K–5) with rooms and what they do.
7. **Safety and check-in** (a dark band): how matching tags work, background checks and ratios only as the church confirms them; an illustration of the name tag and pickup tag.
8. **Students** (Wednesday night) with a teens photo.
9. **Family events** (Trunk or Treat, Advent night, Christmas Eve family service).
10. **For parents**: a note from the family pastor, resources (RightNow Media, parenting classes).
11. **Message** card ("same story upstairs and down"), **pre-register form**, **giving**, wave-topped **footer**.

## Type

Rounded or soft display with a clean text face.
- Fredoka 500–700 + Nunito Sans (reference build).
- Baloo 2 + Figtree; Bricolage Grotesque 800 + Figtree.

## Colour

White ground, navy ink, three bright colours used as fields and shapes, tints for cards, high contrast for text.
- Reference: navy #14204a, sunshine #ffc52e, tomato #f2542d (text version #c23a16), cobalt #1e4fd6, leaf #13774a; tints pink #ffd6cc, light blue #e5edff, light green #ddf4e7, light yellow #fff4cc.
- Alternative: white, navy #1e2a44, cobalt #2350d8 buttons, sun #ffd967, sky #d6ebf7, mint #d8efe2.

## Photos

Families walking together, kids at craft tables, a child praying, teens hanging out, parents and kids at check-in. Arch and circle frames; thick ink outlines are optional.

## Motion

Gently bobbing shapes (paused under reduced motion), stickers that pop in once, wave dividers.

## Signature components

Kids' check-in walkthrough, age-group cards, safety band, pre-register form, family events, parent resources.

## Pitfalls

Childish (clip art, comic fonts, rainbow everything): keep three colours and adult copy; alienating adults without kids (keep an adults/groups path); safety claims the church cannot back up.
